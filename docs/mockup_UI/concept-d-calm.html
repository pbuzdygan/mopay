Concept D · Calm home overview-first, readable for every household member
All concepts

Overview – answers "how are we doing?" in five seconds

  • Information architecture: Overview · Expenses · Incomes · Savings. Today's Reports become Overview and open first. Every current Reports element is kept (income/expenses/net with year-over-year change, month-by-month, where money went, top entries, predictability, savings overview) – Overview shows the summary, month by month, where money went and savings; top entries and predictability move to the Full analysis page (linked, not mocked here).
  • Hero: the current month's net result as the single most important number, in a sentence ("you kept 45% of income"), then the year so far. A marker on each meter shows the monthly average for context (§21 single clear metric).
  • BetterNext step prompt: when a month of the working year has no values yet (here December), the overview says how many entries are missing and offers Fill December, opening Expenses on that month.
  • BetterNotes from tags: all tag notes of the year in one list, newest first, each linking to its cell. Today notes are visible only by hovering individual cells.
  • Warmer neutrals, 15px body text, larger headings and more whitespace – calmer for occasional users, while tables elsewhere stay compact (§35).

Savings – list + detail

  • Goals on the left (master), selected goal on the right (detail) – avoids long accordion pages (§39 list → details).
  • BetterAdd contribution form directly in the goal: amount, note and type (Contribution / Temporary withdrawal). Same savings item model as today; withdrawal = negative amount.
  • Remaining amount and "goals without target" are explicit. Edit/delete goal stay in the header; item actions on hover.

Settings as a page, not a modal

  • Consolidates the hamburger menu: Year operations, Import, Export, Settings, Release info become sections of one page with in-page navigation (§41). Fewer modals, clearer consequences.
  • Existing settings: group totals, view density (Normal/Compact), theme, lock session, release check. Better Theme gains System (follows OS).
  • Import keeps template download, validation and overwrite confirmation (shown as a step flow after choosing a file). Year deletion lives in a separated Danger zone.
  • Demo mode: Year operations and Import sections show a read-only explanation instead of disappearing silently.

PIN screen

  • Centred, quiet unlock: dots grow with input (PIN length 4–8 is not revealed in advance), on-screen keypad for touch, physical keyboard works as usual.
  • Errors are explained in words under the dots (§24), focus stays in the field. Theme follows the saved preference so the screen does not flash.
